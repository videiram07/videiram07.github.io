Imani Wright

"As the older twin constantly put under pressure by other people within the haven of southern Connecticut to be a role model to the other research interns, Imani has learned how to perform extremely well under said pressure in perfect manner to the people surrounding her. Despite being the “little miss perfect” of the two siblings, she cannot show her true self and real emotions to the people around her in fear of upsetting people and the expectations they’ve placed onto her. Previously living in a stronghold protected from the less than fortunate remnants of a zombie apocalypse, the peaceful bubble she lived in is breached, and she’s forced to navigate a new more dangerous world than everyone remembered. There’s no telling if the facade perpetuated by her perfectionism will be maintained or if these new events will break it and force her to become closer with her brother."

Malik Wright

"As the younger twin always lost in his sister’s shadow and never being seen as the most optimal option, Malik possesses a wide range of skills and knowledge of random categories. If he can’t be the best at something, he might as well be decent at multiple things; if he can’t be people’s first option, he could be their second or third. While he may not be favored by everyone, Malik does have a knack for getting along with several types of people and getting them to let their guards down enough to tell the truth. Previously living in a southern Connecticut stronghold protected from the less than fortunate remnants of a zombie apocalypse, the peaceful bubble he lived in is breached, and she’s forced to navigate a new more dangerous world than everyone remembered. In the case of an apocalypse like this, Malik’s random knowledge could save his and his sister’s lives and relationship… or be useless and result in their ends."

Cat Santoso-Lopez

"Years prior to the events of the series, Cat lost someone she loved to something that wasn’t a predicted zombie observed from the original apocalypse. Cat knew she saw something animalistic but couldn’t prove it until she and her younger cousin fled their southern New Mexico stronghold and she saw similar creatures in the escape. While keeping an eye on her younger brother and eventually mediating the disaster piece of the twins’ relationship, she is searching for the answers to what these creatures are, their origins, and why her brother died. Cat is brutally honest with people and isn’t afraid to get her hands dirty, whether to say the stuff no one else wants to say, fix a grim situation, or touch non-metaphorical guts and blood. However, she might have to start curbing her curiosity the closer she gets to the truth and the more danger the people she’s grown attached to get into."

Seb Lopez

"Pushed away by his older siblings and taken under the wing of his older cousin, Seb has been searching for answers to the past along with Cat. Seb was born just as the original apocalypse started and lived in a northern Massachusetts stronghold for most of his life, making him unaware of what zombies or the new animalistic humanoids are until the recent events of the series. While he’s the youngest person in the group and doesn’t have as much life experience as the other members, Seb is astonishingly smart and knows how to keep himself safe from suspicious strangers. He prefers to keep to himself (and Cat) and draw aquatic animals but is a very trusty junior lab assistant. With enough witnessing Cat’s trust in others, Seb will follow her lead to trust them as well. As their journey continues, Seb is forced to witness the cruelty of the world often blocked from his vision by others and possibly the people he’s close to."

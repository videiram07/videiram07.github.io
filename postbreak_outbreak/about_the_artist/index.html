Hi!

I’m 0range (with a zero instead of an “O”!)! This webcomic has been in the works since 2022 with many, MANY revisions. These guys have kind of gone through changes based on my interests of each rewrite— kinda fun tbh. Just so the later chapters don’t come as a surprise, my current main interests include horror movies and games, illustrating, designing, post hardcore and metal music, cool animals, and zombies. ESPECIALLY zombies. For those who are curious, the specifics of the games and movies include the Resident Evil franchise, Scream franchise, Jennifer’s Body, Texas Chainsaw Massacre, and Friday the 13th. Music includes Pierce the Veil, Silverstein, Sleeping with Sirens, Korn, and Linkin Park. While I spend most of my time creating or consuming created media, I sometimes spend time playing open world videogames or hanging out with my friends (who always give me inspiration for my stories).

Find more of my work on Instagram and TikTok!
